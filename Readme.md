# LogReady365

LogReady365 is a simple food truck compliance log app that helps owners replace paper-based hygiene and safety records with digital checklists, timestamped logs, and inspection-ready reports.

## Why this exists

Food truck owners often rely on paper folders, memory, and ad hoc routines for food safety checks. That creates stress, missed logs, and paperwork panic before inspections.

LogReady365 is designed to make compliance faster, simpler, and easier to trust.

## Core MVP features

- Daily opening and closing checks
- Fridge and freezer temperature logs
- Cleaning checklists
- Timestamped digital records
- Inspection-ready report view
- Mobile-first design for use during a shift
